# Getting Started with Aveline

Welcome to **Aveline**, the bespoke intelligence and clienteling concierge engineered for luxury ateliers and fashion boutiques.

Aveline bridges discreet in-store relationship management with multi-agent orchestration to elevate client interactions, curate personalized styling looks, and protect business margins.

---

## Quick Setup Overview

Getting your boutique fully operational takes less than five minutes:

1. **Create an Account:** The boutique owner registers their brand and creates their organizational workspace.
2. **Define Brand Rules:** Set your default currency, bespoke commission thresholds, and agent approval triggers.
3. **Invite Staff & Stylists:** Generate instant, scoped invitation codes so associates can connect immediately.
4. **Download the Mobile Suite:** Equip on-floor associates with the Flutter companion app on iOS or Android.

---

## Inviting Your Team

Team collaboration in Aveline is built around strict tenant boundaries. Each boutique workspace operates within its own cryptographic tenancy:

- **Owners:** Possess full organizational authority, billing oversight, and can adjust financial approval thresholds.
- **Managers:** Coordinate daily atelier shifts, supervise order approvals, and curate seasonal catalog collections.
- **Associates / Stylists:** Build customer relationships, record styling notes, and initiate personalized commerce recommendations.

> **Note on Approvals:** High-value transactions, special discounts exceeding your set policy, or VIP styling holds automatically pause for manager approval before finalizing.

## First Steps in the Concierge

Once you've joined your boutique:

### 1. Register Your First Customer

Use **Ava** to record client dimensions, fabric sensitivities, anniversary dates, and favorite color palettes. Even brief conversational fragments are organized into actionable memory graphs.

### 2. Sourcing & Lookbooks

Let **Elle** analyze garment photographs or digital mood boards. Elle will discover harmonizing inventory and assemble customized lookbooks in seconds.

### 3. Order Drafting & Checkout

When a client selects a collection, **Lina** validates real-time stock availability, applies membership tier discounts, and generates a unified payment link or POS intent.
