# Terms & Privacy

This page summarises the commitments Aveline makes about your account and your clients' data. The
full terms and the privacy notice are published on the Aveline website; where this summary and
those documents differ, those documents govern.

## Who is responsible for client data

For the personal data of your clients, **your boutique is the data controller and Aveline is the
processor**: we handle that data on your instructions and for the purpose of running the service
for you. For your own account data as an owner or staff member, Aveline is the controller.

- Your boutique is responsible for having a lawful basis to hold and use its clients' data.
- Aveline will not use one boutique's client data to serve another boutique, and does not sell
  personal data.
- If a client asks your boutique to stop processing, to correct their record or to delete it, tell
  us and we will help you act on it.

## Accounts, roles and access

Every person in a boutique holds one role: **Owner**, **Supervisor**, **Manager** or **Staff**. The
role decides what they can open and do. Accounts are for adults; you must be at least 18 years old
to hold an owner or staff account. An owner is responsible for the people they invite, the roles
they assign, and removing access when someone leaves.

## Sign-in and credentials

Sign-in is handled by **Clerk**, our identity provider, and **two-factor sign-in is supported**.
Session length is Clerk's; the dashboard sets no idle timeout of its own. Credentials you connect
for WhatsApp, Instagram or a payment gateway are encrypted at rest with **AES-256-GCM** and are
used only to provide the service to your boutique. Fees charged by those providers are yours.

## Availability

We work to keep the service available, but we do not promise uninterrupted or error-free operation,
and we may need to suspend it briefly for maintenance or to protect the service. The service is
under active development: features may change, and a feature described in the documentation may be
adjusted or withdrawn as the product matures.

## Acceptable use, in one paragraph

Use Aveline for lawful boutique operations, and only for the boutique you belong to. Do not try to
reach another boutique's data, do not resell the service, do not send unlawful or unsolicited
messages through a connected channel, and do not upload content you have no right to upload.

## What Aveline does not do

- **No impersonation.** Aveline never pretends to be a staff member or a client.
- **No cross-boutique learning.** Data from one boutique never trains or informs another.
- **No silent export.** Data leaves the workspace only when a person asks for it.

## Ending your use

You may stop using the service at any time. We may suspend or end access if these terms are
breached, if the law requires it, or if an account risks other boutiques or the service. When
access ends, the boutique loses access to the workspace; how long data is kept afterwards is set out
in the privacy notice.
