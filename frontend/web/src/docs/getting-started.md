# Getting Started

Aveline is a boutique concierge: it keeps a memory of every client your team serves, helps you
source and style pieces, receives your customer messages, and keeps a register of what the shop
took. This page walks a boutique owner from an empty account to a running dashboard.

---

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- Your boutique's **name**, **physical address** and a **Sri Lankan phone number** — nine digits,
  written as `+94 77 12 12 123`.
- The **plan** you want to begin on. No payment is collected during setup.
- Any of your own **WhatsApp Business, Instagram or payment-gateway credentials** you want to
  connect now, though you can equally do that later from [Integrations](/docs/integrations).

## Step 1: Create your account

1. Open the Aveline site and click **Create account**.
2. Choose **Boutique owner** — *I run a boutique and want to open it on Aveline.*
3. Fill in **First name**, **Last name**, **Username**, **Email** and **Password**, then accept the
   terms. You can also continue with **Google** or **Facebook**.
4. Click **Create account**. If your email needs verifying, Aveline sends a one-time code — enter it
   under **Verification code** and click **Verify & continue**.

You land in the setup wizard straight away. Nothing is charged at any point.

## Step 2: Choose your role

The first screen asks whether you are opening a boutique or joining one. Choose **I'm a Boutique
Owner** and click **Proceed to Boutique Setup**.

A colleague who was given an invitation code chooses **I'm Staff** instead — see
[Joining a Boutique](/docs/joining-a-boutique).

## Step 3: Tell us about your boutique

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Boutique Name** | Yes | Up to 200 characters. A domain handle is previewed underneath as you type. |
| **Physical Address** | Yes | Up to 500 characters. |
| **Contact Phone Number** | Yes | A Sri Lankan number: nine digits after `+94`. |
| **Boutique Description** | No | Up to 500 characters. Shown on your dashboard. |
| **Logo Image URL** | No | A link to your logo image. |

Click **Continue to Plan Selection**.

## Step 4: Choose a plan

Pick the plan you want to begin on:

| Plan | Price | Blossoms per month | Staff seats | Customers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Seed** | Free | 150 | 1 | 50 |
| **Bloom** | LKR 3,500/mo | 750 | 3 | 250 |
| **Orchid** | LKR 9,000/mo | 2,000 | 10 | 1,000 |
| **Rose** | LKR 20,000/mo | 5,000 | 25 | 5,000 |

No payment or credit card is collected today: your account receives its full Blossom credits
immediately, and the Aveline team contacts you later for payment verification. The plan you choose
sets the limits in the **Plan entitlements** table on [Plan & Billing](/docs/billing), which is
always the authoritative version for your boutique.

## Step 5: Describe your concierge

This is where you tell Aveline how to behave with your clients.

- **Brand Voice & Tone** — choose one of the three presets or write your own.
- **Business & Commerce Rules** — your discount ceiling, delivery terms, and return policy.
  Aveline uses these when a price or a promise is at stake.

What you can set here depends on the plan you chose:

| Plan | What you can configure |
|---|---|
| **Seed** | Nothing on this screen — Aveline runs on standard concierge presets. |
| **Bloom** | Brand voice and business rules. |
| **Orchid**, **Rose** | Brand voice, business rules, preferred colours and fabrics, and client etiquette and memory rules. |

Click **Continue to Integrations**.

## Step 6: Connect your channels (optional)

You can connect WhatsApp Business, Instagram and your payment gateway here, or click **Skip for
now** and do it later from Integrations. Credentials are encrypted and scoped to your boutique
only.

## Step 7: Invite your team (optional)

Generate an onboarding code for a colleague: choose the **Staff Role** (Supervisor, Manager or
Staff) and a **Code Expiration** (24 hours, 7 days or 30 days), and optionally enter their email.
Click **Generate Code**, then copy the code, copy the invitation link, or show its QR code.

You can also do this later from [Team](/docs/team).

## Step 8: Review and launch

Check the summary, then click **Create Boutique**. Aveline activates your boutique, credits your
Blossom allowance and warms up your agents. The success screen confirms your allocation and offers
**Enter Atelier Dashboard**, which is where your work begins.

## Your first hour in the dashboard

1. **Add a piece.** [Catalog](/docs/catalog) is the shop floor: photograph a garment and Aveline
   reads its colour, fabric and style for you.
2. **Add a client.** Open [Customers](/docs/customers), click **Add client**, and record a walk-in.
3. **Log a visit.** Open the client and click **Log a visit**. Only an inbound, in-person
   interaction counts as a visit, and no Blossoms are charged.
4. **Ask Aveline something.** The blossom button in the header opens the concierge chat. The
   [Salon](/docs/salon) is where client threads live.
5. **Look at your takings.** [Income](/docs/income) reports what the shop collected separately from
   what the orders say was sold.

## What your role can do

You are the **Owner** of the boutique: the only role that reaches Billing, Settings and
Integrations. [Roles & Permissions](/docs/roles-permissions) has the full table, and it is what to
read before you invite your first colleague.
