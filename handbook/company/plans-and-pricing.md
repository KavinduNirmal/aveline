# Plans & Pricing

Aveline is offered on four plans. A plan sets a monthly allowance of Blossoms, a number of staff
seats and a limit on active clients.

## The plans

The setup wizard proposes these four plans, with these monthly figures. Prices are **LKR list
prices**.

| Plan | Price | Blossoms per month | Staff seats | Customers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Seed** | Free | 150 | 1 | 50 |
| **Bloom** | LKR 3,500/mo | 750 | 3 | 250 |
| **Orchid** | LKR 9,000/mo | 2,000 | 10 | 1,000 |
| **Rose** | LKR 20,000/mo | 5,000 | 25 | 5,000 |

## The entitlements table is the authority

The **Plan entitlements** card in Billing and in Settings is the authoritative list of what your
boutique has right now, with the source each limit resolved from and the date it took effect. A
plan name is not the same thing as a limit: read the entitlements. A value the server did not
return reads **not measured**, and a missing figure is never drawn as zero.

Entitlements can cover the monthly Blossom allowance, staff seats, active clients, API requests per
month and per minute, WhatsApp messages per month, and how long statistics are kept.

## How payment works today

**No payment provider is connected to the service.** Nothing is charged through Aveline, there is
no checkout, no card is collected and no invoice document is produced. Billing is a statement of
account, not a demand for payment.

Where a plan is not free, the Aveline team contacts you to arrange payment before any charge
applies.

## Changing your plan

Plan changes are arranged with the Aveline team rather than applied instantly, because staff seats,
the Blossom allowance and the billing cycle all move together. The effective date is confirmed with
you before anything changes. Use the Contact page to start one.

The **Upgrade plan** button in Usage and Billing is not a self-service plan change today.

## Blossom top-ups

An owner can record a Blossom top-up in Billing. A top-up is a **recorded grant, not a charge**:
you choose a pack from your boutique's price book, and the payment reference you enter is the only
evidence of payment Aveline stores.
