# Blossoms

A **Blossom** is Aveline's unit of AI work. Instead of asking you to read tokens, model calls or
provider costs, Aveline converts everything its agents do for your boutique into Blossoms. Your
plan grants a monthly allowance, and the Usage section shows what has been spent from it.

## Where to see your balance

The **Blossom balance** card in Usage shows how many Blossoms remain, with the allowance
underneath, and the four parts of the current period's position: **Allowance**, **Granted**,
**Adjusted** and **Used**. Every role can see the balance; the detailed panels are available to a
manager or owner.

If the server did not report a balance, the card says so in words rather than drawing a zero. A
missing figure is unknown, not spent.

## What does not cost Blossoms

**Logging a visit or an interaction does not cost Blossoms.** Recording what happened with a client
is free; the AI work around it is what Blossoms measure.

## Top-ups

An owner can record a top-up in Billing. A top-up is a **recorded grant, not a charge**. Choose a
pack from your boutique's price book and optionally record a payment reference.

## Blossoms expire

**Unused Blossoms expire.** Each entry in the Blossom statement carries an expiry date, and a
balance that is not used within its validity is not carried forward indefinitely. The Usage and
Billing sections show the expiry of any grant you hold; ask support if you need the figure for a
specific period.

## What Aveline does not report

Aveline does not record which person or which client an AI action belonged to, so there is no
"who spent the Blossoms" breakdown. The statement has no per-user column, and there is no agentic
panel showing runs, tokens or provider cost.
