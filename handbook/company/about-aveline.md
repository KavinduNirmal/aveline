# About Aveline

Aveline is boutique concierge software. It gives a fashion boutique one workspace for its client
book, its catalogue, its customer conversations and its takings, and it puts an AI concierge in
that workspace to do the reading, drafting and remembering.

Aveline is an assistant, not an authority. It drafts and suggests; a person at the boutique
decides. Nothing in the product commits the boutique to anything on its own.

## The concierge and the specialists

You talk to **Aveline**, the concierge. Behind her are three specialists, each with a narrow job:

| Specialist | What she handles |
|---|---|
| **Ava** | customer memory: who a client is, what they want, their history and preferences |
| **Elle** | visual work: finding pieces, colours, styles, sizes, stock, styling and sourcing |
| **Lina** | commerce: price, discount, margin, payment, orders and delivery |

A question about a product is Elle's, not Ava's. A question about price or an order is Lina's.
When a request goes past a limit the boutique has set, Aveline stops and asks a person to decide.

## What Aveline does

- Keeps a client book: contacts, interaction history, preferences, events and consent.
- Holds client conversations in one place and receives inbound WhatsApp messages.
- Reads garments from photographs and searches the catalogue by meaning, not just by keyword.
- Drafts styling suggestions and customer replies for a person to review and send.
- Routes discounts and orders that break a rule to a person for approval.
- Reports what the shop took, from the takings register and the Blossom ledger.

## What Aveline does not do

- **It does not give legal, tax, accounting or financial advice.** Its output is not a substitute
  for your own judgement.
- **It does not pretend to be a person.** There is no impersonation of staff or clients.
- **It does not learn across boutiques.** One boutique's client data is never used to serve
  another, and personal data is not sold.
- **It does not export data silently.** Anything that leaves the workspace is something a person
  asked for.
- **It does not take payment.** No payment provider is connected to the service today.
- **It does not show agent internals.** Model calls, tokens and provider costs are converted into
  Blossoms, which is the unit the boutique is billed in.
