# Contact & Support

## Support

Support is by email at **{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}**. We read everything, but **we do not currently offer a
guaranteed response time**. If something is urgent, say so in the subject line and include the
boutique name.

Before you write, it helps to include:

- the section you were in (for example Salon, Catalog or Billing);
- what you expected to happen, and what happened instead;
- the approximate time, so we can find the run in the logs.

## Sales, onboarding and multi-store plans

The same address reaches the team for sales and onboarding. If you run several boutiques, ask about
multi-store plans and dedicated onboarding.

## Where we are

Aveline is built in **Colombo, Sri Lanka**, and serves boutiques across the island.

## What support cannot do for you

Support will not ask for your password, your API keys, or a two-factor code. Aveline staff will
never ask for a credential by email. If a message claims to be from us and asks for one, treat it
as fraudulent and tell us.
